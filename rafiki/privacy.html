Rafiki privacy policy

Rafiki is a private app used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google accounts. No one else's data is requested.

What it accesses

The owner's Google account data they choose to connect, such as Gmail messages and calendar events.

How it is used

Only to give the owner their own features, such as organising bookings and tasks. To do this, some content may be processed by the AI model service the app uses. It is not sold, not shared for advertising, and not used to train general AI models by this app.

Storage

Data is stored on the owner's own computer and phone. Sign-in tokens are kept in the device's secure storage.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings.
